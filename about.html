Who I am

I'm a Software Developer and ML enthusiast based in Pune, Maharashtra — passionate about building systems that work at scale and hold up under pressure. My journey started with a deep curiosity about what happens beneath abstractions, and evolved into shipping production-grade APIs, event-driven architectures, and AI-powered pipelines.

I believe the best engineering is invisible — it just works, fast and reliably, whether it's serving 10 users or 10,000. Every system I build is designed with that principle in mind.

What I build

At Biddano Pvt. Ltd., I've shipped secure RESTful microservices with 99.9% uptime, built WebSocket endpoints handling 50+ concurrent connections with <100ms latency, and optimized database queries for 30% faster data retrieval. I also integrate React frontends with the APIs I build.

Beyond backend, I'm deeply interested in Generative AI — building RAG pipelines, integrating Whisper for speech intelligence, and working with LangChain and HuggingFace to create systems that actually reason about data rather than just storing it.
